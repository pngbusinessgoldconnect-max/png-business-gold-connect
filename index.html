About PNG Business & Gold Connect

A developing platform founded by Ivan Bandi.

PNG Business & Gold Connect aims to make it easier for people in Papua New Guinea to discover business support services and find contact information for businesses and gold-market participants. We are starting with a simple directory and enquiry service, with more features planned as the platform grows.

Transparency: Listings are not endorsements. Users should check businesses, licences, pricing and legal requirements independently. The website does not guarantee a transaction or investment return.
